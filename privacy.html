What we process

  • Account information: email address, account identifier and sign-in provider. The first release uses Google sign-in and, on iOS, Sign in with Apple through Supabase Auth. Email/password registration is disabled. Providers can supply your name and email; your provider password is not given to the game. Apple may provide a private relay email. An Apple authorization refresh grant is encrypted in private server storage to revoke the authorization when you delete your account; it is not sent back to the game client and is removed with the account.
  • Your explorer profile and progress: nickname, selected game avatar, language and presentation preferences, match results and moves, scores, XP, badges, daily progress, saved islands and island designs. Cloud records allow the same account to restore its adventures on another device.
  • Social activity: friend codes, requests, accepted friendships, blocks, shared progress, favorite islands and friend challenges. A recipient of a friend request can see your nickname and avatar; accepted friends can see the progress and favorite island you share. Your email and full private match history are not displayed to friends. There is no free-text chat or photo upload. The optional leaderboard displays the public game profile described below. A studio is visible to accepted friends only after its owner chooses to publish it.
  • Market activity: earned and purchased coin balance, scenery/cosmetic unlocks and market purchases. In versions supporting coin packs, Apple App Store or Google Play handles payment; we do not receive your card or bank details. Store-signed proof is verified by our Supabase backend before coins are added. We retain the product, store, environment, credited amount and a SHA-256 transaction fingerprint linked to your account; raw receipts/purchase tokens are processed for verification without being stored in our database. Refund notifications adjust the coin wallet. Purchased coins do not grant XP or ranking points.
  • Technical information: Supabase, Google and Unity may process connection and service logs, including IP addresses, to provide services and protect against abuse. Authentication sessions are stored using the device's protected credential storage. Gameplay saves and preferences also exist locally on your device.

Advertising partners

Optional videos can be opened from Home or the island Market. Home’s offer can be dismissed for the UTC day. In release builds, completing a daily bonus video gives 20 market coins in v1.13 and later (older versions keep their disclosed 5-coin offer), up to 3 verified rewards per UTC day; only a Google-signed server receipt credits points. An opaque reward ticket links the receipt to your account on our server. Test builds label demonstration videos and never award real video points. Occasional interstitial ads may appear after a newly completed match, when you leave its results. An interstitial can be offered after every two newly completed matches, with at least 90 seconds after any full-screen ad. Availability is not guaranteed. Ads do not appear at launch, during turns, tutorials or replay. If an ad cannot load, play continues. We do not send your email, nickname, Supabase user ID or friend code to the advertising SDK.

In v1.13 and later, a separate optional video can double a verified normal match’s earned coins, once per match and up to three matches per UTC day. Skipping never removes the original reward; XP and leaderboard points are unchanged. Tile-refresh videos reward choices, not coins. We collect limited operational ad measurements: platform, app version, placement, preparation-time bucket, fixed outcome/error category and, if the SDK provides it, estimated impression revenue in currency micros and its precision. We store daily aggregates for up to 90 days, without names, emails, advertising IDs, locations or raw SDK messages. Account-linked batch IDs and rate-limit counts prevent duplicates and abuse and are retained for up to seven days of active ingestion (or deleted with the account). These observations are not authoritative billing records.

All advertising requests use teen-restricted treatment, a general-audience content rating and non-personalized requests. Publisher first-party ID is disabled. The app does not ask for Apple's tracking permission; Android advertising ID and Ad Services permissions are removed. These settings do not mean Google processes no data: its SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, device or app identifiers permitted by the platform/settings, video interactions and diagnostic/performance information. Google describes its processing in its privacy policy and SDK disclosure. Requests also restrict data processing (RDP). From v1.14, the configured US advertising partners are Google and Unity Ads. Privacy choices, when required by Google's consent system, are available in Preferences → Ad privacy.

Age eligibility

The first public release accepts accounts only for players aged 16 or over. A neutral birth-date check occurs on the device before sign-in, account creation, saved-session restoration or advertising requests. The full birth date is neither stored nor uploaded; the device retains only the eligibility result. This is a self-declaration, not verified identity or parental consent. Access for younger players is deferred until an appropriate parental verification process is in place. Conservative advertising settings also apply to adults. There are no gallery uploads, contact imports, precise location requests or direct player messaging. Parents may contact the privacy address about an account created in an earlier private test.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Account-linked game records are retained to support your active account and cross-device restore. Removing the app clears neither your cloud account nor its server records. Signing out also does not delete the account. We retain account-linked records while the account is active and remove them when a verified deletion request is completed. Requests are handled within the deadlines required by applicable law; we may first verify ownership. Provider security logs and any backups may persist for their applicable retention periods and are not used to restore a deleted account for ordinary gameplay. We do not promise that uninstalling erases provider logs or backups immediately.

You may request access, correction or deletion of account data, and raise a privacy concern, using the public contact address above. Depending on your location, other rights may apply. Verification may be needed to protect another player's data. In v0.13 and later, delete your account in Profile → Settings → Delete my account. The app asks for confirmation and a sign-in session opened within the last ten minutes. Deletion revokes account sessions and removes the account, cloud game records, friendships and reward ledger, along with the current device’s cached adventures. Older private test versions use the contact address to request deletion. Offline copies on other devices cannot be erased remotely; reconnect or remove their local app data. We do not claim automatic deletion on uninstall.

Quests, cosmetics and rankings

Daily quest reward receipts, earned XP, milestone progress, selected avatar frames/pins and purchased cosmetics are stored with your private game account for restoration and duplicate-reward prevention. Island Coins are earned game currency, with no cash value. All accounts automatically participate in the world leaderboard. Signed-in players see only the Top 50 explorer names, bundled avatars and server-verified lifetime duel scores. An account identifier supports reporting and blocking, but no email or match record is displayed. The list has no participation switch. Accepted friends can compare their lifetime scores; blocked explorers are excluded. Spending coins does not reduce your lifetime score.

Community safety

Profile and island names are filtered. In-app reports contain the reporter account, reported account, category and a snapshot of the reported name and shared island; these records are private to support and used to review abuse. Reported users cannot see who reported them. You can report and block from a friend profile or report a shared island. Contact evrenayan@gmail.com for support. Reports are removed when either associated account is deleted.

Coin purchase records

Deleting your account removes its coin wallet and the link between you and purchase records. Anonymous transaction fingerprints, product/amount and refund state remain to prevent the same paid transaction being claimed again. Apple and Google retain their payment records under their own policies. Coins have no cash value, cannot be transferred or cashed out, and do not expire while your account remains active. Coin packs are one-time consumable purchases, not subscriptions. Store refunds can reduce your spendable balance; if refunded coins were already spent, later coin earnings first cover that shortfall. The payment providers handle refund requests.

Unity Ads mediation from v1.14

AdMob may request a contextual video or interstitial from Unity Ads as an additional ad source. Google-signed server verification remains required for game rewards. Before initialization, we pass false for Unity's GDPR advertising consent and US privacy consent settings; we do not claim consent to personalized ads or sale/sharing. Optional Unity Developer Data optimization is not enabled, and the game does not forward its account identity or match history to Unity Ads. The advertising SDK may nevertheless process device/app identifiers allowed by the platform and settings, IP-based approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic information for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. No tracking permission is requested, and Android advertising-ID/Ad Services permissions remain removed. Unity provides its own privacy notice and player data choices: Unity Privacy Policy. Provider-level requests are separate from deleting your Island Makers account.

Unity’s SDK disclosure also lists store purchase history as collected and shared for advertising and analytics. This SDK processing is separate from our server-side purchase verification. It does not include payment-card or bank-account details.

Version 1.15 adds a decoration project with a known reward: complete three verified duels, or optionally add one step with a completed video per UTC day. Unfinished progress persists. Collection is limited to one project per UTC week. Project videos grant steps, not coins; duplicate owned decorations become 20 coins. Project and verified video receipts are private account records and are deleted with the account. Market-context videos use the existing three daily 20-coin bonus quota. After privacy checks permit ads, native video inventory may be preloaded; no reward entitlement is created until you request a video.

We use coarse daily counts of game interactions and move-confirmation latency to improve play. These aggregate counters contain no player identifier. Private retry and abuse-prevention records expire after seven days and are removed during regular maintenance. We calculate first and second match completion and day-one/day-seven return rates from existing server-backed match and daily-visit records. Designated test and review accounts are excluded. No additional analytics SDK is used.

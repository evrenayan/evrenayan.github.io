Island Makers · Privacy policy

This notice describes the current test release. Live monetization is not enabled.

English

Effective date: 6 October 2026. Controller: Evren Ayan; contact: evrenayan@gmail.com. Privacy and account deletion contact: evrenayan@gmail.com.

What we process

  • Account information: email address, account identifier and sign-in provider. Email/password accounts and Google sign-in are handled by Supabase Auth. Google sign-in can provide basic profile information, such as your name and email. Your Google password is not given to the game.
  • Your explorer profile and progress: nickname, selected game avatar, language and presentation preferences, match results and moves, scores, XP, badges, daily progress, saved islands and island designs. Cloud records allow the same account to restore its adventures on another device.
  • Social activity: friend codes, requests, accepted friendships, blocks, shared progress, favorite islands and friend challenges. A recipient of a friend request can see your nickname and avatar; accepted friends can see the progress and favorite island you share. Your email and full private match history are not displayed to friends. There is no public player directory or free-text chat in this beta.
  • Market activity: earned point balance, scenery unlocks and purchases made with game points. This beta does not process real-money purchases.
  • Technical information: Supabase and Google may process connection and service logs, including IP addresses, to provide services and protect against abuse. Authentication sessions are stored using the device's protected credential storage. Gameplay saves and preferences also exist locally on your device.

Optional videos and Google

Videos are optional and are not shown automatically during a match or at launch. The current beta uses Google demonstration ads; watching them does not earn spendable points or advertising revenue. If verified live rewards are enabled in a later version, an opaque, single-use reward ticket and the advertising transaction will be used to validate the reward. We do not send your email, nickname, Supabase user ID or friend code to the advertising SDK.

All advertising requests in this beta use child-directed treatment, a general-audience content rating and non-personalized requests. Publisher first-party ID is disabled. The app does not ask for Apple's tracking permission; Android advertising ID and Ad Services permissions are removed. These settings do not mean Google processes no data: its SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, device or app identifiers permitted by the platform/settings, video interactions and diagnostic/performance information. Google describes its processing in its privacy policy and SDK disclosure. Privacy choices, when required by Google's consent system, are available in Preferences → Ad privacy.

Why and with whom

We use these records to operate sign-in, save and restore your game, provide the requested friend features, maintain your earned-point inventory and prevent duplicate or fraudulent rewards. Supabase provides authentication and database services; Google provides Google sign-in, consent management and optional ads. Apple/TestFlight and Google Play operate their distribution services under their own policies. Service providers may process data outside your country. The game database is hosted in Supabase’s London (United Kingdom) region. Provider support and security processing may take place in other locations. We process account and game data to provide the service you request; service security and reward verification protect the game against abuse. Where applicable law requires consent, it must be obtained before the corresponding processing. Provider transfer safeguards are described in the Supabase data processing addendum and the Google privacy policy.

Young players

Island Makers is designed for ages 10 and above. Advertising is treated conservatively for all players, including adults. There are no gallery uploads, contact imports, precise location requests or direct player messaging. The current release is a private testing beta, not a public children’s service. If you are below the age at which you may authorize an account in your country, a parent or guardian must contact us before the account is used. A parent can use the privacy email below to ask about a child’s account or request its removal. The beta does not include an automated parental-consent verification system. Child-directed ad settings alone do not replace the permissions required for children’s accounts; this must be resolved before a public launch to young players.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Account-linked game records are retained to support your active account and cross-device restore. Removing the app clears neither your cloud account nor its server records. Signing out also does not delete the account. We retain account-linked records while the account is active and remove them when a verified deletion request is completed. Requests are handled within the deadlines required by applicable law; we may first verify ownership. Provider security logs and any backups may persist for their applicable retention periods and are not used to restore a deleted account for ordinary gameplay. We do not promise that uninstalling erases provider logs or backups immediately.

You may request access, correction or deletion of account data, and raise a privacy concern, using the public contact address above. Depending on your location, other rights may apply. Verification may be needed to protect another player's data. The beta has no self-service in-app account deletion screen yet; requests must be handled by the operator. We do not claim automatic deletion on uninstall.

Changes

We will update this notice when our data practices change and publish the effective date. Contact the controller above about this policy.

Genç oyuncular

Oyun 10 yaş ve üzeri için tasarlanmıştır. Yetişkinler dahil tüm oyuncuların reklamları korumalı ayarlarla istenir. Galeri yükleme, rehber aktarımı, hassas konum ve oyuncular arası doğrudan mesaj yoktur. Mevcut sürüm özel bir test betasıdır; halka açık bir çocuk hizmeti değildir. Ülkenizde hesabınıza izin verme yaşının altındaysanız hesap kullanılmadan önce ebeveyniniz veya vasiniz bizimle iletişime geçmelidir. Ebeveynler aşağıdaki gizlilik adresinden çocuğun hesabı hakkında bilgi veya silme talep edebilir. Betada otomatik ebeveyn rızası doğrulaması yoktur. Çocuk reklam ayarları, çocuk hesapları için gereken iznin yerine geçmez; genç oyunculara halka açık yayından önce bu süreç tamamlanmalıdır.

Saklama, silme ve tercihleriniz

Hesap kayıtları hesabınıza hizmet vermek ve cihazlar arası geri yükleme için tutulur. Uygulamayı kaldırmak veya çıkış yapmak bulut hesabını ve sunucu verilerini silmez. Hesaba bağlı kayıtlar hesap aktifken saklanır ve doğrulanmış silme talebi tamamlanınca kaldırılır. Başvurular ilgili mevzuatın süreleri içinde ele alınır; önce hesap sahipliği doğrulanabilir. Sağlayıcı güvenlik kayıtları ve varsa yedekler, ilgili saklama dönemlerinde kalabilir; silinmiş hesabın normal oyun için yeniden oluşturulmasında kullanılmaz. Uygulamayı kaldırınca bu kayıtların hemen silindiği sözü verilmez. Yukarıdaki iletişim adresinden erişim, düzeltme ve silme başvurusu yapabilirsiniz. Konumunuza göre başka haklarınız bulunabilir; hesap sahipliğini doğrulamak gerekebilir. Betada uygulama içinden hesap silme ekranı henüz yoktur; talepleri işletmeci ele alır. Uygulamayı kaldırınca otomatik silme sözü verilmez.

Vídeos opcionales

No mostramos anuncios automáticamente al iniciar ni durante una partida. La beta usa anuncios de demostración de Google; no generan puntos gastables ni ingresos. Si una versión posterior activa recompensas verificadas, se usarán un ticket aleatorio de un solo uso y la transacción publicitaria para validar la recompensa. No enviamos al SDK publicitario tu correo, apodo, identificador de Supabase ni código de amistad.

Todas las solicitudes usan tratamiento infantil, clasificación para todos los públicos y anuncios no personalizados. El identificador propio del editor está desactivado. No pedimos el permiso de seguimiento de Apple; los permisos de ID publicitario y Ad Services de Android se han eliminado. Esto no significa que Google no trate datos: puede tratar ubicación aproximada derivada de la IP, identificadores permitidos por la plataforma/configuración, interacción con vídeos y datos de diagnóstico/rendimiento. Consulta la política de Google y su información del SDK. Cuando el sistema de consentimiento lo requiera, las opciones aparecen en Preferences → Ad privacy.

Finalidades y proveedores

Usamos los datos para el acceso, guardar/restaurar el juego, las funciones de amistad solicitadas, el inventario de puntos y evitar recompensas duplicadas o fraudulentas. Supabase presta servicios de autenticación/base de datos; Google, acceso, consentimiento y anuncios opcionales. Apple/TestFlight y Google Play aplican sus políticas de distribución. Los proveedores pueden tratar datos fuera de tu país. Tratamos datos de cuenta y juego para prestar el servicio solicitado y protegerlo frente al abuso. Si la ley exige consentimiento, debe obtenerse antes del tratamiento correspondiente. Consulta el acuerdo de tratamiento de Supabase y la política de Google.

Jugadores jóvenes

El juego está pensado para mayores de 10 años. Aplicamos la configuración publicitaria infantil también a adultos. No hay carga de fotos, importación de contactos, solicitud de ubicación precisa ni mensajes directos. Esta versión es una beta privada de pruebas, no un servicio público infantil. Si no tienes la edad para autorizar una cuenta en tu país, un padre o tutor debe contactarnos antes de usarla. Puede pedir información o eliminación de la cuenta a través del correo de privacidad. No hay verificación automática de consentimiento parental en la beta. La configuración publicitaria infantil no sustituye los permisos para cuentas de menores; este proceso debe completarse antes del lanzamiento público para jugadores jóvenes.

Conservación y derechos

Conservamos los registros para atender la cuenta y restaurarla en otros dispositivos. Desinstalar o cerrar sesión no elimina la cuenta ni sus datos de la nube. Conservamos datos vinculados a la cuenta mientras está activa y los eliminamos al completar una solicitud verificada. Atendemos solicitudes en los plazos de la ley aplicable y podemos verificar la titularidad. Los registros de seguridad y posibles copias de los proveedores pueden persistir durante sus periodos de conservación; no se usan para restaurar una cuenta eliminada en el juego. Desinstalar no borra inmediatamente estos registros. Puedes pedir acceso, rectificación o eliminación usando el contacto anterior. Puede haber otros derechos según tu ubicación y puede ser necesario verificar la titularidad. La beta aún no tiene eliminación de cuenta dentro de la app; el operador debe tramitar las solicitudes. No prometemos eliminación automática al desinstalar.

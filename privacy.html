Island Makers · Privacy policy

This notice covers the v0.14 release candidate and earlier private tests. Test builds use demonstration ads; public release builds may use live advertising. The first public release is restricted to accounts aged 16 and over.

Effective date: 6 October 2026. Controller: Evren Ayan; contact: evrenayan@gmail.com. Privacy and account deletion contact: evrenayan@gmail.com.

What we process

  • Account information: email address, account identifier and sign-in provider. The first release uses Google sign-in and, on iOS, Sign in with Apple through Supabase Auth. Email/password registration is disabled. Providers can supply your name and email; your provider password is not given to the game. Apple may provide a private relay email. An Apple authorization refresh grant is encrypted in private server storage to revoke the authorization when you delete your account; it is not sent back to the game client and is removed with the account.
  • Your explorer profile and progress: nickname, selected game avatar, language and presentation preferences, match results and moves, scores, XP, badges, daily progress, saved islands and island designs. Cloud records allow the same account to restore its adventures on another device.
  • Social activity: friend codes, requests, accepted friendships, blocks, shared progress, favorite islands and friend challenges. A recipient of a friend request can see your nickname and avatar; accepted friends can see the progress and favorite island you share. Your email and full private match history are not displayed to friends. There is no public player directory or free-text chat in this beta.
  • Market activity: earned point balance, scenery unlocks and purchases made with game points. This beta does not process real-money purchases.
  • Technical information: Supabase and Google may process connection and service logs, including IP addresses, to provide services and protect against abuse. Authentication sessions are stored using the device's protected credential storage. Gameplay saves and preferences also exist locally on your device.

Advertising and Google

Optional videos can be opened from Home or the island Market. Home’s offer can be dismissed for the UTC day. In release builds, completing a rewarded video can give 5 market points, up to 3 verified rewards per UTC day; only a Google-signed server receipt credits points. An opaque reward ticket links the receipt to your account on our server. Test builds label demonstration videos and never award real video points. Occasional interstitial ads may appear after a newly completed match, when you leave its results. The first two duels of a session are ad-free; the app requests a placement every third duel, with at least three minutes between interstitials and a five-per-day placement cap. Ads do not appear at launch, during turns, tutorials or replay. If an ad cannot load, play continues. We do not send your email, nickname, Supabase user ID or friend code to the advertising SDK.

All advertising requests use child-directed treatment, a general-audience content rating and non-personalized requests. Publisher first-party ID is disabled. The app does not ask for Apple's tracking permission; Android advertising ID and Ad Services permissions are removed. These settings do not mean Google processes no data: its SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, device or app identifiers permitted by the platform/settings, video interactions and diagnostic/performance information. Google describes its processing in its privacy policy and SDK disclosure. Requests also restrict data processing (RDP). US advertising partners are limited to Google. Privacy choices, when required by Google's consent system, are available in Preferences → Ad privacy.

Why and with whom

We use these records to operate sign-in, save and restore your game, provide the requested friend features, maintain your earned-point inventory and prevent duplicate or fraudulent rewards. Supabase provides authentication and database services; Google provides Google sign-in, consent management and optional ads. Apple also provides Sign in with Apple; Apple/TestFlight and Google Play operate distribution under their own policies. See the Apple privacy policy. Service providers may process data outside your country. The game database is hosted in Supabase’s London (United Kingdom) region. Provider support and security processing may take place in other locations. We process account and game data to provide the service you request; service security and reward verification protect the game against abuse. Where applicable law requires consent, it must be obtained before the corresponding processing. Provider transfer safeguards are described in the Supabase data processing addendum and the Google privacy policy.

Age eligibility

The first public release accepts accounts only for players aged 16 or over. A neutral birth-date check occurs on the device before sign-in, account creation, saved-session restoration or advertising requests. The full birth date is neither stored nor uploaded; the device retains only the eligibility result. This is a self-declaration, not verified identity or parental consent. Access for younger players is deferred until an appropriate parental verification process is in place. Conservative advertising settings also apply to adults. There are no gallery uploads, contact imports, precise location requests or direct player messaging. Parents may contact the privacy address about an account created in an earlier private test.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Account-linked game records are retained to support your active account and cross-device restore. Removing the app clears neither your cloud account nor its server records. Signing out also does not delete the account. We retain account-linked records while the account is active and remove them when a verified deletion request is completed. Requests are handled within the deadlines required by applicable law; we may first verify ownership. Provider security logs and any backups may persist for their applicable retention periods and are not used to restore a deleted account for ordinary gameplay. We do not promise that uninstalling erases provider logs or backups immediately.

You may request access, correction or deletion of account data, and raise a privacy concern, using the public contact address above. Depending on your location, other rights may apply. Verification may be needed to protect another player's data. In v0.13 and later, delete your account in Explorer → Preferences → Delete my account. The app asks for confirmation and a sign-in session opened within the last ten minutes. Deletion revokes account sessions and removes the account, cloud game records, friendships and reward ledger, along with the current device’s cached adventures. Older private test versions use the contact address to request deletion. Offline copies on other devices cannot be erased remotely; reconnect or remove their local app data. We do not claim automatic deletion on uninstall.

Changes

We will update this notice when our data practices change and publish the effective date. Contact the controller above about this policy.

Hesap kayıtları hesabınıza hizmet vermek ve cihazlar arası geri yükleme için tutulur. Uygulamayı kaldırmak veya çıkış yapmak bulut hesabını ve sunucu verilerini silmez. Sağlayıcı güvenlik kayıtları ve varsa yedekler, ilgili saklama dönemlerinde kalabilir; silinmiş hesabın normal oyun için yeniden oluşturulmasında kullanılmaz. Uygulamayı kaldırınca bu kayıtların hemen silindiği sözü verilmez. Yukarıdaki iletişim adresinden erişim, düzeltme ve silme başvurusu yapabilirsiniz. Konumunuza göre başka haklarınız bulunabilir; hesap sahipliğini doğrulamak gerekebilir. v0.13 ve sonrasında Explorer → Preferences → Delete my account yoluyla hesabınızı silebilirsiniz. Önceki özel test sürümlerinde iletişim adresinden silme talep edilir. Diğer cihazlardaki çevrimdışı kopyalar uzaktan silinemez; yeniden bağlanın veya yerel uygulama verilerini kaldırın. Uygulamayı kaldırınca otomatik silme sözü verilmez.

Conservación y derechos

Conservamos los registros para atender la cuenta y restaurarla en otros dispositivos. Desinstalar o cerrar sesión no elimina la cuenta ni sus datos de la nube. Conservamos datos vinculados a la cuenta mientras está activa y los eliminamos al completar una solicitud verificada. Atendemos solicitudes en los plazos de la ley aplicable y podemos verificar la titularidad. Los registros de seguridad y posibles copias de los proveedores pueden persistir durante sus periodos de conservación; no se usan para restaurar una cuenta eliminada en el juego. Desinstalar no borra inmediatamente estos registros. Puedes pedir acceso, rectificación o eliminación usando el contacto anterior. Puede haber otros derechos según tu ubicación y puede ser necesario verificar la titularidad. En v0.13 y posteriores puedes eliminar tu cuenta en Explorer → Preferences → Delete my account. Se pide confirmación y una sesión iniciada en los últimos diez minutos. Se revocan las sesiones y se eliminan la cuenta, datos de juego en la nube, amistades, recompensas y aventuras guardadas en este dispositivo. En pruebas anteriores se solicita por correo. Las copias sin conexión de otros dispositivos no se borran remotamente: reconecta o elimina sus datos locales. No prometemos eliminación automática al desinstalar.

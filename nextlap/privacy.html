Privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Applies to Next Lap — Swim Manager, package com.nextlap.game. Publisher: Evren Ayan. Contact: evrenayan@gmail.com.

This policy describes the mobile game’s data practices. The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. Default swimmers, including characters aged 10–17, are fictional. Optional personalization lets you supply club and swimmer names and reference race times. Simulated health and mood indicators remain game variables, not medical measurements or advice.

Services and sharing

Supabase provides authentication, database and server functions. Apple and Google provide optional sign-in and store services. Google AdMob and its consent tools provide advertising, including mediated Unity Ads where enabled. Relevant providers process data under their own policies, potentially in countries different from yours: Supabase, Apple and Google and Unity Ads.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your account profile to build a personalized advertising audience. The app requests teen treatment, disables publisher first-party IDs and requests non-personalized advertising. Where required and available, advertising privacy controls can be opened from the in-game shop. The game does not request access to your contacts, microphone or precise location.

When you choose a rewarded ad, we send Google AdMob your Next Lap account ID and a one-time reward-session reference to verify and credit the reward. This is not your password or email address.

Private club friendlies

Optional invitation-code friendlies share your club name, an age group/category and an anonymous simulated swim with the invited club. Real swimmer names, uploaded logos and manually entered race references are not shared. A code expires after three days; match snapshots are retained for up to 30 days. You can report and block a club from Friendly → Safety, or contact evrenayan@gmail.com. Blocks last until you remove them or delete your account; reports are retained as needed for support review and removed on account deletion. No public player directory or chat is provided.

Invitation rules: only invite people you know. Abusive, hateful, sexual or harassing club names and behaviour are prohibited. Display names are filtered; reports are reviewed by support and offending access may be restricted.

Local reminders

Optional club reminders are scheduled on your device at 08:00 and 20:00 Europe/Istanbul in your selected game language. Enabling them requires operating-system notification permission. You can disable them in Preferences. No push token is uploaded and the reminders do not send your club data to a notification service.

Images and device storage

Club memory images are generated on your device. Saving an image to Photos uses the operating system’s permission and happens only when you choose Save Image. Sharing uses your device’s share flow. Next Lap does not upload these images to its servers. If you choose a club logo, the system picker grants access to only your selected image. The app crops it to 256 × 256 pixels and the server removes image metadata before saving it in private account-linked storage. Logos are cached on your device, are accessible through authenticated requests for your own account, and are removed during account deletion. Sign-in tokens are held in the device’s protected credential store; local career files are used for continuity and pending synchronization.

Retention, deletion and your choices

We keep account-linked data while your account is active to provide the game. You can edit your profile and preferences and delete your account in the app or request deletion by email. Deletion removes active account-linked records and invalidates sessions; temporary provider backups and technical logs expire through their normal retention cycles. Store providers may retain their own transaction or security records for legal and operational reasons. If automatic Apple authorization revocation is unavailable, the app asks Apple-linked users to remove Next Lap in Apple Account → Sign in with Apple after deletion.

To request access, correction, export or deletion of personal data, contact the support email from your account’s address. We verify ownership and respond under applicable law. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or identity documents. If you believe a child below 13 has created an account, contact us so we can investigate and remove the account where appropriate.

Security and updates

Connections use HTTPS, account data is access-controlled and purchases are validated by the server. No service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy as the game changes; the effective date will be revised here.

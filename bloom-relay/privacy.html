Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026 · Developer: Evren Ayan

Türkçe özeti

Bloom Relay saves game progress on your device and does not require a game account. Native Android and iOS builds can include rewarded advertising and occasional completed-puzzle interstitial ads through Google AdMob and, where configured, Unity Ads mediation. Those advertising services can process device and usage data.

What the game stores

The game stores puzzle progress, earned virtual bouquets, unlocked tools and decorations, planted seeds and growth, weekly garden missions, seasonal flower collections, discovery routes, greenhouse layouts and per-pot flower choices, visitor journals, flower recipes, mastery goals, hint usage, reward receipts, challenge progress, ad-frequency counters and your language, sound, animation and vibration preferences locally. These records support saving, restoring and avoiding duplicate game rewards. Bloom Relay does not upload these game saves to a developer-operated account or game server.

Your operating system may include local app data in device backups according to your device settings. Clearing app data or removing the app can remove local progress. Platform backup copies are controlled by your platform provider.

Greenhouse postcards

Postcards are created from your in-game greenhouse and stored temporarily in the app cache. The game does not access your camera or photo library to create them. Only when you choose Share does it pass the selected PNG to your device’s share sheet. You select the destination; that service handles the shared image under its own policies. Bloom Relay does not upload postcards to a developer server.

Advertising

When the native advertising feature is initialized, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and, where configured in mediation, Unity Ads may process IP address and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Provider data can also include SDK-assigned user identifiers, advertising conversion or purchase-related information, app usage times and device characteristics such as model, language and connection type, as described in the providers’ SDK disclosures. This does not mean Bloom Relay offers in-app purchases or uploads your game progress. Their purposes can include delivering and measuring ads, analytics, SDK functionality, security and fraud prevention. Choosing non-personalized ads does not mean no data is processed.

Ads and consent forms require an internet connection. Consent information is refreshed before requesting ads, and required privacy messages are presented through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Ads are requested only when UMP permits them. The game requests non-personalized Google ads and applies restrictive sharing choices for Unity Ads. Availability and data handling depend on platform, region, device permissions and your choices. If a provider privacy form does not support your device language, its default English version may be displayed.

Rewarded videos are optional. You can play the core game without accepting a rewarded offer. Occasional interstitial ads may appear when leaving a completed-puzzle result after its celebration. The first ten campaign puzzles are protected; later automatic ads require four distinct completions and at least three minutes since the first observation or last opened full-screen ad. If an ad is unavailable, the game continues. An extra game reward is issued only after the ad provider confirms that it was earned. Ads are unavailable in the desktop/macOS version and Unity Editor.

Your privacy choices

When required by the consent platform, use the advertising privacy choices option in game settings to reopen the privacy form. You can also use your device's advertising privacy and permission controls. Refusing or withdrawing consent may limit available ads. We do not treat permission to request an ad as permission for personalized advertising.

Ad providers' privacy information and rights mechanisms are available here:

  • Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.
  • Unity Privacy Policy and Unity game player and app user privacy information.

Accounts, contacts and children

The game does not ask for your name, email address, date of birth, contacts, photos or precise GPS location, and it has no game account system or player chat. Bloom Relay is intended for players aged 16 and over and is not primarily directed at children. This intended audience does not replace the formal age rating assigned by each app store; follow the rating and applicable rules in your region. We do not invite children to submit personal information through support.

Delete data, retention and requests

  1. Local game data: no Bloom Relay account exists to delete. On Android, open device Settings → Apps → Bloom Relay → Storage → Clear storage/data. On iPhone or iPad, delete Bloom Relay using Delete App (offloading alone may retain data). These actions remove the local game save, rewards, settings and cached postcards from that installation. This cannot be undone unless you have a device backup.
  2. Backups: manage or delete operating-system backup copies through your Apple or Google backup settings. Reinstalling or restoring a backup can restore old local records.
  3. Advertising records: use the privacy/data controls in the ad, the in-game advertising privacy choices when available, and the Google and Unity privacy-policy links above to submit access or deletion requests directly to the provider holding those records. Do not put advertising/device identifiers in public support issues. Provider retention periods and legal exceptions are described in their policies; deleting the app does not by itself erase their server records.
  4. Help: email evrenayan@gmail.com with “Bloom Relay privacy” if you need help finding the appropriate route. We do not hold a central game-save database or offer an account-deletion form.

Local game data remains on your device until removed or replaced. Advertising partners determine retention of data they process under their policies; use their linked privacy controls for requests about their records. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data. We cannot look up device-only game saves on a server.

This website and support

This static website uses no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, forms or cookies added by Bloom Relay. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process connection information such as IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link brings you to that service's policies.

For questions about this policy, contact Evren Ayan through the public support tracker or the public developer profile. Support issues are public: do not post personal, sensitive or device-identifying information. Information you choose to send is used to address your request and is also processed by the service you use.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when the game's data practices change. Any new consent required for changed advertising practices will be requested through the applicable privacy flow.
